# Visual provenance

Business logos were supplied by the owner. Top Clas and Baby 4 You use black vector recreations. The parent logo selectively recolours the supplied green artwork to burgundy.

All visible catalogue photographs are newly AI-generated apparel visuals matched to the collection descriptions. Original uploaded product photographs are no longer referenced in the website. Generated photographs do not confirm stock, exact fabric, size assortment or packing; these are confirmed through an enquiry.

The hero, About and brand campaign photographs are AI-created editorial visuals, with no printed model numbers or brand lettering. They are not photographs of the office or customer children. No Pinterest or Unsplash photos are used.

Each catalogue entry has an AI-created outfit photograph matched to its garment reference as gallery image one, and the existing garment-only reference as image two. These are campaign visualisations; availability and exact stock details remain confirmed on enquiry. The adult selection uses an adult model.
